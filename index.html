07 / A few useful answers

Before we
get started.

The essentials about starting, choosing a program and working online.

Read all frequently asked questions →
Who can consult Art of Eating?

Support is available for people seeking practical everyday nutrition, metabolic and hormonal health guidance, menopause support or sports and fitness nutrition. Choose an area of care above, or ask the team which consultation fits your needs.

What should I have ready for the first consultation?

Be ready to discuss your goals, everyday meals, work or training routine, food preferences and relevant medical care. The team will tell you which recent reports, medication details or other assessment information they need and how to share them securely.

Can my plan include South Indian food and family meals?

Yes. Familiar foods, cultural preferences and the practicalities of cooking at home belong in the planning conversation. Discuss eating out, travel, vegetarian choices and overseas ingredient availability so the plan reflects your daily life.

How much do programs cost, and are taxes included?

The six program cards list the duration, included support and total charges with 18% GST. Ask the team to confirm the current fee and accepted payment methods before enrolment.

Can you help with tournaments, travel and supplement questions?

These are part of the sports nutrition services. Discuss your competition calendar, travel and food access, recovery needs and any supplements with the team. Supplements are not mandatory for everyone; recommendations depend on your individual context.
