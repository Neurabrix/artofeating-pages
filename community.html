Women’s health & community

Menoparty

An experiential gathering for women in perimenopause, menopause and post-menopause, bringing 200 participants together to learn, connect and celebrate this phase of life.

Conversations span sexual health, fitness, gynaecology, dermatology, nutrition, bone health and mental health. Singing, dance, art therapy and community interaction make room for expression as well as learning.

Menopause is a phase to understand, embrace and approach differently.

Professional dialogue

Obesity 360

A two-hour multidisciplinary session exploring obesity and metabolic health from a 360° perspective. Approximately 100 participants joined, including physiotherapists, junior doctors, psychologists, dietitians, fitness trainers and enthusiasts.

Seven specialists brought their perspectives to panel discussions and case-based learning, connecting nutrition with endocrinology, orthopaedics, bariatric care, gynaecology, sports medicine, fitness and sports psychology.
