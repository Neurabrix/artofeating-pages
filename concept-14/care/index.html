05 / NRI hub · Art of Eating worldwide

A little closer to home.
Wherever you live.

Living overseas doesn’t have to mean leaving familiar food behind. Explore online nutrition support built around the ingredients, routines and food choices available to you.

  1. Tell us where you live. Share your country, time zone and goals.
  2. Agree your next steps. The team confirms availability, assessment and a suitable program.
  3. Build and review your plan. Work with locally available foods and the follow-ups included in your program.
  • Indian food, adapted overseas
  • Everyday grocery substitutions
  • Vegetarian food planning
  • Travel and remote follow-ups
